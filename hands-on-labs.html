INTERACTIVE TEACHING SIMULATION

Generate. Observe. Explain.

This is a calculated classical signal model, not a connected instrument or a qubit. Readouts are ideal predictions; hardware noise, loading and acquisition artifacts are not simulated.

Waveform generator

CH1 reference = 0°. When CH2 is enabled it shares frequency, amplitude and offset; its phase is adjustable.

Oscilloscope

Scale readouts appear at the top, as on the physical scope. CH1 is the trigger reference.

How to use the digital instruments
  1. Select an experiment preset, then change one generator control.
  2. Keep scope scales fixed when comparing wave height or timing.
  3. For Burst: choose 1–4 cycles, press scope SINGLE, then generator TRIGGER. The captured trace freezes.
  4. For phase: enable CH2 and move its phase slider. CH1 stays fixed.
  5. For interference: enable Math. At equal sine amplitudes, 0° reinforces and 180° cancels.

The simulated screen has 10 horizontal and 8 vertical divisions. Values outside the chosen voltage range are clipped; increase V/div to see them. STOP holds the previous capture while controls change.
