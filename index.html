FIRST, UNDERSTAND THE DIFFERENCE

From a classical bit to a quantum qubit

No advanced physics required. Discover what changes, what stays the same, and why the Bloch sphere helps explain the science.

0 OR 1

Classical bit

A definite logical value: 0 or 1. Ordinary computers process many classical bits using logic operations.

Think of an OFF/ON switch—an introductory analogy.
→
α|0⟩ + β|1⟩

Qubit

A quantum state with two complex probability amplitudes. Gates transform amplitudes and phase. A Z measurement still returns either 0 or 1.

The full Bloch sphere and its measurement controls appear in your first lesson.

Quantum computing is not simply doing every classical calculation faster. Its special algorithms use quantum amplitudes and interference for certain tasks.
