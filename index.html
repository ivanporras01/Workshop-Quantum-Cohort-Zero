Quantum Industry News

Recent announcements from major quantum companies and research teams. Last reviewed November 12, 2026.

IBM · Nov 11, 2026 · Roadmap

Nighthawk r3 targets 520 programmable qubits

IBM says its next-generation processor is designed for 10,000 quantum operations across 520 programmable qubits. It plans to connect two r3 processors in 2027.

Discuss in class. Which numbers describe a design target, and which describe a future system?

IBM press release ↗ The Quantum Insider coverage ↗

Rigetti · Nov 10, 2026 · Hardware benchmark

Rigetti reports 99.5% median two-qubit gate fidelity

Rigetti reported this benchmark on its 108-qubit superconducting system. Gate fidelity measures how closely an operation matches the intended one.

Rigetti press release ↗

D-Wave · Nov 10, 2026 · Hardware delivery

An Advantage2 annealing system arrives in Italy

D-Wave’s quarterly update reported delivery of a 4,400-plus-qubit Advantage2 system for installation in Como. Quantum annealers are designed for a different style of computation than gate-based processors.

D-Wave press release ↗

QuEra · Nov 10, 2026 · Research

AI-assisted methods explore lower resource requirements

QuEra, Harvard and Anthropic announced research using AI to find ways to reduce the resources needed for certain quantum applications.

Joint press release ↗

Quantinuum · Nov 5, 2026 · Error correction

Helios update highlights logical-qubit experiments

Quantinuum reported new error-correction results on its trapped-ion Helios system. A logical qubit uses an encoding across physical qubits to protect information.

Quantinuum press release ↗

IonQ · Nov 5, 2026 · Hardware deployment

IonQ reports Tempo deployments

IonQ highlighted deployments of its Tempo quantum computers in its quarterly update. Tempo uses trapped-ion qubits.

IonQ press release ↗

Amazon Web Services · Oct 16, 2026 · Cloud access

Amazon Braket enters FedRAMP Class C scope

AWS announced that its own Braket service components are covered by this authorization boundary. Third-party quantum hardware sits outside that boundary.

AWS announcement ↗

Google Quantum AI · Oct 15, 2026 · Error correction

Google tests magic-state cultivation on Willow

Google reported about 40 times fewer errors in a magic-state preparation experiment on Willow compared with its approach without cultivation. Magic states help implement operations used in fault-tolerant quantum circuits.

Google research announcement ↗
